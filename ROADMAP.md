# Roadmap

## 1.1 - Connected collection

- OBS Cost Details Export reader with schema versioning
- Billing Center pagination and retry adapter
- AOM/CES metric collector
- CCE workload-to-node cost allocation
- collection freshness and lineage metrics

## 1.2 - Decision workflow

- recommendation lifecycle persistence
- owner approval and expiring exceptions
- SMN notification routing
- realized savings ledger
- SLO/error-budget validation hooks

## 1.3 - Advanced intelligence

- hierarchical product/service forecasting
- seasonality and launch-event features
- regional price catalog adapter
- commitment coverage simulation
- Pareto optimization for cost, carbon proxy, and reliability

## 2.0 - Governed execution

- signed action plans
- canary rightsizing workflow
- automatic rollback on SLO regression
- policy-bounded non-production scheduling
- immutable audit evidence

No execution automation will be enabled without explicit approval boundaries,
least-privilege credentials, and tested rollback.
